## Executive summary

> **Goal.** State what Stage 3-A changed (grammar default, diagnostics API, reproducible driver), what it
> proves, and what it deliberately does *not*.

- **Grammar cleanup (systems).** The two Stage-2.5 safety constraints — `goal_predicate_relevance` (goal
  literals only over the domain's goal-schema predicates; for Gripper-lite that whitelist is `("at_ball",)`)
  and `require_goal_var_connected` (every goal-literal variable must also occur in the rule's action
  arguments or a positive state precondition — the *local* connectedness rule of 02.md §H4) — are now the
  **default** of `LiftedGrammarConfig` (they were default-OFF ablations in Stage 2.5). Consequence: the two
  goal-literal artifacts that the Stage-2 "best" uniform-MCTS solver leaned on — a vacuous goal predicate
  `Goal[carrying(?b_0)]` and a disconnected auxiliary variable in `Goal[at_ball(?aux_0, ?r_1)]` — are now
  *unreachable by construction*, while all four Stage-1 hand-policy rules remain expressible up to
  α-renaming. The MCTS action-head width for the Gripper-lite signature drops from $M=13$ (legacy) to
  $M=7$ (strict). `legacy_grammar_config()` / `strict_grammar_config()` name the two presets; the Stage-2/2.5
  canonical-run generator (`make_lifted_gripper_canonical.py`) is pinned to `legacy` so [02.md](02.md)'s
  Table 1 numbers stay reproducible.
- **Per-policy pathology report (systems).** A new pure function
  `lifted_diagnostics.analyze_policy_pathologies(policy, *, goal_predicate_names)` reports, for any complete
  lifted policy, the booleans `has_vacuous_goal_predicate`, `has_goal_only_variable`, `has_empty_body_rule`,
  `has_top_{drop,pick,move}_rule` and the counts `num_goal_literals`, `num_negative_goal_literals`,
  `num_rules`, `num_body_literals`. This dict is attached to **every** evaluated-policy JSONL record written
  by the smoke script and the new diagnostic-grid driver.
- **Reproducible diagnostic-grid driver (systems).** `run_lifted_gripper_diagnostic_grid.py` runs a
  `(run ∈ {A, B}) × (grammar ∈ {legacy, strict}) × (baseline ∈ {uniform-MCTS, uniform-random}) × seed × sims`
  grid; each cell writes a config JSON (with the grammar-config dataclass dump + git commit), a best-so-far
  JSONL, a summary JSON (incl. aggregate pathology counts and root visit-count entropy for MCTS cells), the
  full per-policy JSONL (under `results/…` — gitignored / regenerable), and a row in a rolled-up CSV. The
  uniform-random baseline draws complete derivations uniformly at random until its unique-policy count
  matches the paired MCTS cell. No claim-bearing log is left only in `/tmp`.
- **What Stage 3-A does *not* do / claim.** No neural policy/value network (that is H5 in 02.md). No change
  to the Stage-1 interpreter semantics. No claim that the strict grammar fixes the deceptive sparse-reward
  landscape — the `⊤ ⇒ drop` "do-nothing" attractor and the very low solver density both survive (see
  [02.md](02.md) Table 2 `r3 both`; the small grid below confirms it). The small committed grid (seeds 0-1,
  sims 64, 8 episodes) is a smoke/illustration grid — it is *not* powered to compare uniform-MCTS against
  uniform-random solver rates, and the full seed × sims sweep was **not run** (Appendix C).

## Minimal setup

> **Goal.** Pin the new objects: the two grammar presets, the three "grammar artifacts", and the pathology
> report. Everything else (the task family $\mathcal{M}_B$, the leaf utility $U_B$, the derivation game
> $\mathcal{D}_\Gamma$) is exactly as in [02.md §Minimal setup](02.md#minimal-setup).

**Grammar presets.** Two named `LiftedGrammarConfig` constructors
([lifted_grammar.py](../../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_grammar.py)):

$$
\Gamma_{\mathrm{strict}} = \mathrm{cfg}[\texttt{goal\_predicate\_relevance}{=}\mathsf{T},\ \texttt{require\_goal\_var\_connected}{=}\mathsf{T}], \qquad
\Gamma_{\mathrm{legacy}} = \mathrm{cfg}[\dots{=}\mathsf{F},\ \dots{=}\mathsf{F}].
$$

$\Gamma_{\mathrm{strict}}$ is the new dataclass default. The draft-name mapping (kept for the
code-fidelity audit): `goal_predicate_relevance` $\equiv$ `restrict_goal_predicates_to_goal_schema`;
`require_goal_var_connected` $\equiv$ `require_goal_literal_vars_bound`; its negation $\equiv$
`allow_goal_only_existential_vars`. The goal-predicate whitelist lives on `DomainSignature`
(`gripper_lite_signature().goal_predicate_names = ("at_ball",)`), not on the config.

**Grammar artifacts (what the pathology report names).** Three structural features of a complete policy
$\pi \in \Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}}$ that the Stage-2 / 2.5 landscape study flagged as spurious-solver
enablers on Gripper-lite (the first two are removed by $\Gamma_{\mathrm{strict}}$; the third is reported,
not forbidden):

1. **vacuous goal predicate** — a goal literal whose predicate $\notin$ `sig.goal_predicate_names` (e.g.
   `Goal[carrying(...)]`, `Goal[handempty()]`). Removed by `goal_predicate_relevance`.
2. **goal-only variable** — a variable bound only inside a goal literal (no action argument, no positive
   state literal). Removed by `require_goal_var_connected`.
3. **⊤-headed action rule** — a body-less rule $\top \Rightarrow a$. The $\top \Rightarrow \mathrm{drop}$
   instance is the "do-nothing" attractor of 02.md; a body-less `move` can be a legitimate default, so the
   grammar does *not* forbid these — the pathology report just counts them.

**Pathology report.** The map
$\texttt{analyze\_policy\_pathologies} : \Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}} \times (\Sigma_{\mathrm{goal}} \cup \{\bot\}) \to (\text{bool}^{7} \times \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^{4})$
of [lifted_diagnostics.py](../../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_diagnostics.py); pure, reusing
`rule_has_vacuous_goal_predicate` / `rule_has_disconnected_goal_var` from `lifted_grammar.py`. When
`goal_predicate_names` is $\bot$ (`None`), `has_vacuous_goal_predicate` is always `False` (no notion of
"vacuous" for the domain) — the disconnected-aux flag is structural and reported regardless.

## Systems result

> **Goal.** Tie each Stage-3-A systems claim to a passing test, so the executable status is auditable
> without re-reading the code.

1. **Strict grammar is the default; hand policy survives it.** Every Stage-1 hand-policy rule is reachable
   up to α-renaming under the default (strict) config — `tests/test_lifted_grammar.py::test_hand_policy_still_expressible_after_goal_filters`,
   `::test_current_grammar_still_expresses_hand_policy_rules`, `::test_grammar_can_express_hand_policy_rules`
   (the last two now exercise the strict grammar, since it is the default).
2. **The two goal-literal artifacts are unreachable under the strict default.** No `goal_lit` hole offers a
   `carrying`/`handempty` goal literal — `::test_gripper_goal_literals_only_use_goal_predicates`. A goal
   literal cannot introduce a goal-only auxiliary variable — `::test_positive_goal_literal_cannot_introduce_goal_only_aux_var`.
3. **The brittle Stage-2 solver is no longer generable.** Its ρ₁ (`Goal[at_ball(?aux_0, ?r_1)]`) is rejected
   by `require_goal_var_connected` and its ρ₂ (`Goal[carrying(?b_0)]`) by `goal_predicate_relevance`; its ρ₃
   (`⊤ ⇒ move`) remains generable — `::test_spurious_runA_solver_no_longer_expressible`.
4. **Safe negation is unchanged.** Still enforced at the grammar level and again in `Rule.__post_init__` —
   `::test_safe_negation_at_grammar_level` (now under the explicit permissive grammar),
   `::test_safe_negation_still_enforced` (under the strict default).
5. **Action-head bound is still a valid upper bound under every flag combination** —
   `::test_compute_max_productions_is_a_real_upper_bound`, `::test_compute_max_productions_is_valid_under_constraints`
   (the strict-default value for the Gripper-lite signature is $M = 7$; the legacy value is $13$).
6. **Per-policy pathology report.** `analyze_policy_pathologies` returns exactly the documented key set;
   it flags the brittle Stage-2 policy (vacuous + goal-only-var), the degenerate `⊤ ⇒ drop` policy
   (empty-body + top-drop), and reports the hand policy clean; pure / deterministic — `tests/test_lifted_diagnostics.py`.
7. **Reproducible diagnostic-grid driver.** A miniature grid (1 seed, sims 8, 1 episode, run A, strict
   grammar, both baselines) produces `config.json` (with the grammar-config dump + git commit), `best.jsonl`,
   `summary.json`, the full `all.jsonl` (with the pathology dict on every record), and a rolled-up CSV with
   the documented header — `tests/test_run_lifted_gripper_diagnostic_grid.py::test_diagnostic_grid_smoke`.
8. **MCTS / interpreter / Stage-1 untouched.** `LiftedDerivationGame`, `MCTS`, `UniformPolicyValueNet`,
   `interpret`, and the Gripper-lite env were not edited; `LiftedLeafEvaluator` gained only an additive
   accessor for the cached `Policy` objects. Full suite: `pytest tests/ -q --ignore=tests/test_zoning_game.py`
   → **1197 passed, 2 skipped** (Stage-2.5 baseline 1185; +12 net new tests, no regressions).

## Interpretation

> **Goal.** State the one thing Stage 3-A establishes, with the usual *(Non-claim.)* / *(Limit.)* tags.

*(Systems.)* The strict grammar is now the default, it removes the two **goal-literal** artifacts by
construction (Fig 4, `::test_gripper_goal_literals_only_use_goal_predicates`,
`::test_positive_goal_literal_cannot_introduce_goal_only_aux_var`), it makes the specific brittle Stage-2
solver non-generable (`::test_spurious_runA_solver_no_longer_expressible`), and the four hand-policy rules
survive it (`::test_hand_policy_still_expressible_after_goal_filters`). Every evaluated policy now carries a
structural pathology report, and the diagnostic-grid driver writes fully-provenanced (config + git + JSONL +
summary + CSV) baseline runs — the reproducibility hole from Stage 2 (claim-bearing logs only in `/tmp`) is
closed.

*(Limit.)* This is **not** a fix for the deceptive sparse-reward landscape. The $\top\Rightarrow\mathrm{drop}$
attractor is untouched (Fig 4 right; the small grid's `best score` $= -0.05$ everywhere), and the strict
grammar's smaller action space does not raise solver density at $R=3$ (02.md Table 2 `r3 both` already showed
$<0.1\%$). *(Non-claim.)* the small committed grid (seeds 0-1, sims 64, 8 episodes) finds zero solvers — at
this depth/budget that is the expected outcome on *either* grammar, so it says nothing about whether uniform
MCTS beats uniform random, nor about the strict grammar's effect on search. The structural change is the
deliverable; the empirical comparison needs the full grid (Appendix C) and, downstream, the learned prior
(H5 in 02.md).

## Appendix C — what remains untested

- **The full diagnostic sweep** — 10 seeds × {64, 128, 256, 512} sims × {legacy, strict} × {A, B} × {MCTS,
  random}, 64 episodes per MCTS cell. The driver supports it; it was *not run* here (running it and reporting
  numbers would be an unrun claim). Until then: no powered uniform-MCTS-vs-uniform-random comparison, no
  $\mathrm{HitRate}_B(N)$ curve, no entropy-vs-budget trend.
- **Whether the strict grammar raises MCTS HitRate at realistic budgets.** Stage 2.5 showed grammar
  restriction multiplies the *static* $B=1$ solver count $\approx 4\times$ in a $5{,}000$-policy sample and
  yields the only $B{=}1\to B{=}2$ generaliser; whether that translates into faster discovery by uniform
  MCTS is exactly what the full sweep would measure.
- **Any learned policy/value network.** That is H5 / Stage 3 — out of scope for Stage 3-A by design.
