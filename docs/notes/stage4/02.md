## Executive summary

> **Goal.** State Stage-2's load-bearing systems claims, what they do *not* prove, the one-sentence negative finding, and what Stage 2.5 adds on top.

- **What Stage 2 proves (systems).** The typed lifted grammar at [lifted_grammar.py](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_grammar.py) generates complete, well-typed lifted policies; it expresses every rule of the Stage-1 hand policy up to α-renaming; the unchanged MCTS engine drives the new [`LiftedDerivationGame`](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_derivation.py) and scores leaves through the Stage-1 `interpret` via [`LiftedLeafEvaluator`](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_leaf_evaluator.py). Run A (seed 0 / 128 sims) discovers eight $B=1$ solvers in 4,726 distinct policies.
- **What Stage 2 does *not* prove.** Learned-AlphaZero superiority; multi-seed reliability of MCTS at any cell; existence of an MCTS-found $B=2$-generalizing policy; that the "best learned policy" is the hand policy (it is *not* — its $B=1$ solve relies on a vacuous goal literal `Goal[carrying(?b_0)]` and a disconnected auxiliary variable in `Goal[at_ball(?aux_0, ?r_1)]`).
- **Negative finding (one sentence).** Uniform-prior MCTS faces a **deceptive program-search landscape** on Gripper-lite: the $\top \!\Rightarrow\! \mathrm{drop}$ "do-nothing" attractor and the family of vacuous-goal / disconnected-aux policies occupy the overwhelming mass of the search space and consistently mislead the backed-up-value signal at every seed and budget we tried.
- **What Stage 2.5 adds.** Two *default-off* grammar-safety constraints (`goal_predicate_relevance`, `require_goal_var_connected`) and a reproducible **landscape-enumeration / sampling driver** that quantifies — for the first time — solver density, $B{=}1 \!\to\! B{=}2$ generalisation rate, vacuous-goal/disconnected-aux fractions, and score-shape sensitivity over the program space. Under the combined safety flags (`both` setting, max_rules=3, 5 000 sampled policies) the $B=1$ solver count **rises 4×** (1 → 4) and a $B{=}1 \!\to\! B{=}2$ generaliser appears, while the vacuous-goal and disconnected-aux pathologies are eliminated by construction.

## Minimal setup

> **Goal.** Pin every symbol Tables 1–3 and the figures will use: the task family $\mathcal{M}_B$, the grammar-generated policy class $\Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}}$, the per-instance leaf utility $U_B$, the training/held-out objectives $J_{\mathrm{train}}/J_{\mathrm{out}}$, and the hit-rate $\mathrm{HitRate}_B(N)$.

**Task family.**

$$
\mathcal{M}_B = \langle\mathcal{S}_B, \mathcal{A}_B, T_B, R_B, H_B, s^0_B, G_B\rangle
$$

is the deterministic finite-horizon Gripper-lite MDP with $B$ balls, two rooms, one robot,
horizon $H_B = 4B+4$ ([env.py:44–66](../../../src/alphazeropp/instances/gripper_lite/env.py#L44-L66)); the
full formal definition is in [00 §3](00_draft_lifted_policy_az.md#3-the-task-mdp-gripper-lite).
Task-level grounded actions are written $a \in \mathcal{A}_B$; the legal subset at state $s$ is $\mathcal{A}_B(s)$.

**Grammar config.**

$$
\mathrm{cfg} = (R, L_S, L_G, V_{\mathrm{aux}}) = (\texttt{max\_rules},\;\texttt{max\_pre\_literals},\;\texttt{max\_goal\_literals},\;\texttt{max\_aux\_vars})
$$

plus the two default-off Stage-2.5 toggles `goal_predicate_relevance` and `require_goal_var_connected`
([lifted_grammar.py:48–76](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_grammar.py#L48-L76)).
Stage-2 canonical values: $L_S=3,\,L_G=1,\,V_{\mathrm{aux}}=1$; $R{=}3$ for Run A, $R{=}4$ for Run B.

**Grammar-generated policy class.** The set of complete lifted policies the grammar can produce under `cfg`:

$$
\boxed{\;\Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}} \;=\; \{\, \pi : \pi = \mathrm{to\_program}(s_T),\;\;s_T \in \mathcal{T}(\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}) \,\}\;}
$$

where $\mathcal{T}(\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg})$ is the set of terminal derivation states the typed
attributed grammar reaches under `cfg` ([lifted_derivation.py:142–146](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_derivation.py#L142-L146)).

**Per-instance leaf utility.** Scores one policy on one Gripper-lite instance by rolling it out through the Stage-1 `interpret` to horizon and combining four rollout statistics:

$$
U_B \;:\; \Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}} \;\to\; \mathbb{R}
$$

$$
\boxed{\;U_B(\pi) \;=\; \mathrm{solve}_B(\pi) \;+\; 0.25\,\mathrm{progress}_B(\pi) \;-\; 0.01\,\overline{\mathrm{steps}}_B(\pi) \;-\; 0.05\,\mathrm{noops}_B(\pi)\;}
$$

(implemented in [lifted_leaf_evaluator.py](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_leaf_evaluator.py)). $\mathrm{solve}_B$ is the indicator that the rollout reached $G_B$; $\mathrm{progress}_B$ is the goal-`at_ball` recall at the terminal state; $\overline{\mathrm{steps}}_B$ is the rollout length; $\mathrm{noops}_B$ counts the steps where `interpret` returned `None`. Range under default weights: $U_B(\pi) \in [\,-0.05\!\cdot\!H_B,\;1.25\,]$.

**Training & held-out objectives.** Averages of $U_B$ over the training and held-out ball counts respectively:

$$
\boxed{\;J_{\mathrm{train}}(\pi) \;=\; \frac{1}{|\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{train}}|}\sum_{B\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{train}}} U_B(\pi),
\qquad
J_{\mathrm{out}}(\pi) \;=\; \frac{1}{|\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{out}}|}\sum_{B\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{out}}} U_B(\pi)\;}
$$

The MCTS leaf reward is exactly $J_{\mathrm{train}}(\pi)$ aggregated over `--train-balls` (Stage-2 default: $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{train}}\!=\!\{B_{\mathrm{train}}\}$, a single ball count); $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{out}}\!=\!\{B_{\mathrm{eval}}\}$ is held out.

**Hit rate.** Probability that an MCTS run finds a $\mathcal{M}_B$-solver within its first $N$ distinct evaluated policies:

$$
\boxed{\;\mathrm{HitRate}_B(N) \;=\; \Pr_{\mathrm{seed}}\bigl[\,\exists\, k \le N : \pi_k\ \text{solves}\ \mathcal{M}_B\,\bigr]\;}
$$
estimated over independent MCTS runs (here three seeds at $B=1$ / 128 sims — Stage-2.5 clarification);
$\pi_k$ is the $k$-th distinct evaluated policy in discovery order ($k = \mathrm{first\_solver\_index}$
in `data/summary.json`). Today's estimate is point-only (3 seeds).

**Notation.** $p \in \mathrm{LiftedProduction}$ is a grammar production (an action of the *synthesis* MDP);
$\mathcal{P}(s)$ is the set of legal productions at derivation state $s$; the MCTS action-head width is
$M(\mathrm{cfg}, \Sigma) = \max_s |\mathcal{P}(s)|$. Task-level
notation ($a$, $\mathcal{A}_B$) is reserved for grounded Gripper-lite actions. The MCTS exploration
constant $c=1.5$ is unrelated to `cfg`.

## Interpretation

> **Goal.** State the one substantive finding the figures and tables support — a deceptive sparse terminal-reward landscape in program space — and decompose it into three measurable pieces, with explicit *non-claim* and *limit* tags.

The Stage-2 systems claims (executable grammar, hand-policy reachability, unchanged MCTS engine, end-to-end
evaluator wiring) hold across every committed run and every Stage-2.5 constraint setting (Table 1, §Systems).

*(Interp.)* The substantive failure is a **deceptive sparse terminal-reward landscape in program space**.
The landscape diagnostics quantify three load-bearing pieces of that picture:

1. *(Comp.) Solver density is brutally low.* Even after the two safety flags, fewer than 0.1% of randomly sampled
   3-rule policies solve $B=1$, and fewer than 0.05% solve $B=2$ (Table 2, `r3 both`).
2. *(Comp. + Interp.) Pathologies dominate the default grammar.* In 5 000 randomly sampled default-grammar policies, 87.7%
   use a vacuous goal predicate (`Goal[carrying(...)]` or `Goal[handempty()]`) and 16.2% have a goal
   variable bound only inside the goal literal *(Comp.)*. The Stage-2 "best learned" policy (Run A seed 0 / 128 sims)
   exhibits *both* pathologies *(Emp., Fig 4)*. The `both` setting eliminates them by construction
   without losing the hand policy *(Comp. + Math.)*.
3. *(Comp. + Interp.) Reward shape is not load-bearing relative to the prior.* The five score-variant alternatives all pick
   the same argmax inside the `both`-restricted r3 sample (Table 3) *(Comp.)*, and the degenerate ⊤⇒drop policy
   remains at the ≈96th percentile under every variant *(Comp., Fig L2 right)*. The choice between "solve+progress−steps−noops" and
   "solve+progress" is a small lever *(Interp.)*; the big lever is the *grammar's support* (which pathologies are even
   reachable) and, downstream, a learned prior over productions *(Interp., motivates H5)*.

*(Non-claim.)* The Stage-2 "more search does not help" observation — Run A seed 0 / 512 sims finds zero solvers vs eight
at 128 sims — is preserved as **observed at that seed/budget/grid**, not promoted to a universal claim. The
seed-1 and seed-2 cells at the same 128 budget *both* find zero solvers ([02_landscape_first_solver_index.png](figures/02_landscape_first_solver_index.png)),
which is consistent with (1)–(3) above and confirms that the seed-0 / 128 cell is the *outlier*, not a
robust capability. *(Limit.)* A full $\mathrm{HitRate}_B(N)$ curve over a wider seed grid is the natural next
diagnostic; Stage 3 is the structural fix.

## Hypotheses and next experiments

> **Goal.** Catalogue the five hypotheses the Stage-2.5 results bear on, with the artefact each cites and the next-stage experiment each motivates. Distinguishes hypotheses *partially measured* here (H1, H2, H4) from those *only framed* (H3, H5).

| | hypothesis | Stage-2.5 evidence | next experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** *solver density* | The deceptive-landscape effect is dominated by raw solver density: the rate $\lvert\{\pi : \mathrm{solve}_B(\pi)=1\}\rvert\,/\,\lvert\Pi_{\Gamma,\mathrm{cfg}}\rvert$ is too low for unguided search at typical $R$. | Table 2 quantifies it (≤ 0.1% across all r3 settings). | Sweep $R\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ in the landscape script with stratified sampling to estimate density vs depth; compare to MCTS HitRate at the same depth. |
| **H2** *attractor / reward design* | The −0.05 do-nothing attractor + the +0.25·progress shaping make the gradient point toward "stop without noop" instead of "pick → move → drop". | Table 3 shows `progress_only` does not break the tie for ⊤⇒drop; under `current` it sits at the 96th percentile (vs the 99th percentile under `none`). | A *terminal-bonus-only* score (`solve`-only, no shaping, no penalties) is the next natural ablation; report degenerate rank and B-solver count under it. |
| **H3** *$B=1$ identifiability* | Training on $B=1$ only is genuinely ambiguous: many spurious policies (vacuous goal, disconnected aux) hit $\mathrm{solve}_1$ without learning the pick→move→drop relational chain. | Run A seed 0's best policy is itself such a spurious solver (see Appendix A); landscape `goal_predicate` setting still has 3/5 000 B=1 solvers that don't generalise. | Train MCTS on the *union* $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{train}} = \{B{=}1, B{=}2\}$ and report HitRate per $B$; the joint constraint should excise spurious solvers. |
| **H4** *grammar connectedness* (local rule) | Forcing every goal-literal variable to be bound elsewhere in the rule strictly improves the program space without losing the hand policy. | Implemented as `require_goal_var_connected` (local rule, not transitive — see [02_plan2.md](02_plan2.md) refinement table B.2). r3 `connected` and `both` settings produce the only B=1→B=2 generaliser observed; pathology fraction drops 16% → 0%. **Local-rule deviation.** A literal transitive-closure-from-action-args reading rejects hand-policy ρ₂ (`?aux_0` co-occurs with no action arg), but the Stage-2.5 draft requires ρ₂ to remain expressible. The implemented rule — "every goal-literal variable occurs in the action arguments or in some positive state literal" — preserves ρ₂ via `carrying(?aux_0)` and still blocks the disconnected-aux pathology. | Stronger ablation: a *strict transitive* connectedness with an explicit ρ₂ exception, vs the local rule — does it improve B=2-solver density further without forbidding hand policies? |
| **H5** *learned prior* | A learned policy/value network over `lifted_encoding.py`'s AST will bias the MCTS prior away from vacuous / disconnected / do-nothing productions, making the dense solver region reachable in realistic budgets. | Not yet tested. Stage 2.5 frames it: Tables 2–3 show that grammar restriction alone gets us 4× more B=1 solvers but only 1 B=2 generaliser in 5 000 samples — the next gain must come from a non-uniform prior. | Stage 3: replace `UniformPolicyValueNet` with a learned net, hold the grammar at `LiftedGrammarConfig(goal_predicate_relevance=True, require_goal_var_connected=True, max_rules=4)`, target the $B{=}1 \to B{=}2$ generalisation rate as the load-bearing metric. |

## Appendix B — the derivation game, formally

> *Expanded standalone treatment — the four-level `z`/`q`/`p`/`x` dictionary, the worked linear derivation, the typed-attributed-CFG view, and $B_\rho(x,G)$ — is in [notes/derivation_game.md](notes/derivation_game.md).*

The package's `Game` protocol ([core/game.py](../../../src/alphazeropp/core/game.py)) is single-player and
MCTS-driven (`reset` / `step` / `get_action_mask` / `hashable_obs` / `stash_state` / `clone`).
`LiftedDerivationGame` instantiates it over the grammar: a play starts at the empty derivation, builds rules
one production at a time, and ends when the policy is complete; the only non-zero reward is the leaf
$U_B$ of [Minimal setup](#minimal-setup). The state-machine and action-set figures D1–D3
([02_derivation_state_machine.png](figures/02_derivation_state_machine.png),
[02_derivation_production_sets.png](figures/02_derivation_production_sets.png),
[02_derivation_example.png](figures/02_derivation_example.png), regenerated by
[plot_derivation_game_diagrams.py](../../../scripts/plotting/plot_derivation_game_diagrams.py)) are read
directly from the grammar.

**State space and terminal set.**

$$
s = (\rho_{1:k},\ \mathrm{partial},\ \mathrm{hole}(s)), \qquad
\mathrm{hole}(s) \in \{\texttt{policy}, \texttt{action\_schema}, \texttt{aux\_var}, \texttt{pre\_lit}, \texttt{goal\_lit}, \bot\}
$$

A play starts at $s_0 = ((),\,\texttt{None},\,\texttt{policy})$; the terminal set is $\mathcal{T} = \{s : \mathrm{hole}(s) = \bot\}$.

**Action set and mask.**

$$
p = (\mathrm{holeKind},\ \mathrm{label},\ \mathrm{payload}) \in \mathrm{LiftedProduction}, \qquad
\mathcal{P}(s) = \texttt{enumerate\_productions}(s,\ \mathrm{cfg},\ \Sigma)
$$

The MCTS action-head width is $M(\mathrm{cfg},\Sigma) = \max_s |\mathcal{P}(s)|$ — for the default
Gripper-lite signature, $M = 13$; under `goal_predicate_relevance=True`, $M$ shrinks because the
goal-literal hole offers fewer candidates.

**Transition and reward.**

$$
\delta(s,p) = s.\texttt{apply}(p),
\qquad
r(s, p) = \begin{cases} U_B\bigl(\texttt{to\_program}(\delta(s,p))\bigr) & \delta(s,p) \in \mathcal{T} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
$$

**Observation length** ([lifted_encoding.py:34-39](../../../src/alphazeropp/synthesis/lifted_encoding.py#L34-L39))
$L(\mathrm{cfg}) = 7\bigl(R(1+L_S+L_G+V_{\mathrm{aux}})+1\bigr)$; with default $L_S{=}3,\,L_G{=}1,\,V_{\mathrm{aux}}{=}1$ that is 133 at $R{=}3$ and 175 at $R{=}4$. Uniform MCTS ignores the observation; it becomes load-bearing at Stage 3.

**MCTS selection.** PUCT, $\mathrm{UCB}(s,p) = \hat{Q}_{\mathrm{norm}}(s,p) + c\,\pi_0(p\mid s)\,\sqrt{N(s)}/(1+N(s,p))$, $c=1.5$, $\pi_0$ uniform ([mcts.py:400-447](../../../src/alphazeropp/core/mcts.py#L400-L447)) — *all* search structure under Stage-2/2.5 comes from the backed-up $U_B$ values, which is precisely why a deceptive landscape (Tables 2–3) defeats it.

## Appendix E — refinement deltas vs the plans

(See [02_plan2.md](02_plan2.md) refinement table for the full audit; the load-bearing items.)

- **`require_goal_var_connected` is a *local* rule, not a transitive closure.** The Stage-2.5 draft (§B.2) asked for transitive connectedness from action arguments; literally implemented, that rejects hand-policy ρ₂ (which the draft also requires to remain expressible). The local rule "every goal-lit variable is in the action arguments ∪ positive state literals" satisfies both requirements; this deviation is documented here, in `02_plan2.md`, and inside the helper's docstring.
- **`num_binding_attempts` is now a deprecated alias for `num_rule_evals`.** The committed `*_scores.csv` schema is unchanged (never carried this column); JSONL outputs gain `num_rule_evals` alongside.
- **`DomainSignature.goal_predicate_names`** is a new optional whitelist; `gripper_lite_signature()` sets it to `("at_ball",)`. `None` (default) makes `goal_predicate_relevance=True` inert.
- **Seeds 1 and 2 of Run A @ 128 sims** are now canonical (user's Stage-2.5 clarification); `make_lifted_gripper_canonical.py --skip-slow` regenerates them in ~3-4 min total.
- **r1 exhaustive + r3 sampled × 4 constraint settings** are the committed landscape configs (user's Stage-2.5 clarification); JSON + capped CSV both under `data/`.
